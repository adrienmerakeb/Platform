Off-duty periods

Add holidays, seasonal closure, or special days off. Use “More detail” to specify time of day as well.

Name my Virtual Queue

Leave empty to auto-generate “My Virtual Queue 1”, “My Virtual Queue 2”, etc.

A clear name (e.g. “Main Entrance — Weekdays”) makes it easier to manage multiple queues later.

Queue configuration mode

Select whether you want to start from a standard template or configure everything manually.

Standard mode: Choose from curated templates such as museums, restaurants, clinics, or guided tours. All fields are pre-filled but stay completely editable.

Manual settings:Start with a blank configuration and build your queue from scratch.

Standard Virtual Queues

Choose a template that best matches your activity.

Select a preset to see its description and underlying parameters.

Logic type

These options are always visible. In “Standard” mode they follow your preset;in “Manual” mode you can edit them.

Pure “first come, first served” logic. Ideal for walk-in traffic and continuous flows.

Volume per time-slot

Use “one at a time” for appointments like medical visits. Use “more than one at a time” for flows like museum entries, where several visitors can enter per time-slot.

Standard time-slot duration

Optional but recommended when you want estimated entry times.

The duration of a “standard” slot is used to forecast waiting times and to spread bookings more evenly across the day.

Display estimated entry time

Estimated entry time is calculated as: last entry time + (pending tickets × standard time-slot).Showing it makes visitors feel more in control, but it’s still an estimate.

Allowed bookings

For “Only from my list”, you’ll be able to upload or edit a list of allowed visitor emails. This can also be managed later from “Manage my Virtual Queues”.

Synchronize with my calendar

Connect your personal calendar so that your busy times become “unavailable” slots in this queue’s calendar.

When connected, In-Time will periodically read your “busy” time slots and automatically block them in the queue calendar, preventing double booking.

Standard slots for Mixed mode

Define up to 10 “standard slot types” combining a name, volume and duration. These will be used in Mixed queues to combine live-flow with fixed appointments.

Examples:
Quick visit: 2 persons / 20 min
Guided tour: 10 persons / 90 min

Important:
When a fixed appointment is active, the live flow for this queue is temporarily put on hold for the duration of the appointment and resumes once the meeting is over. Visitors will be informed when the virtual live flow is paused and when it resumes after the appointment is marked as finished or its end-time is reached.

Allowed visitors list

Paste or type one email per line. Only these visitors will be able to book this queue when “Only from my list” is selected.
