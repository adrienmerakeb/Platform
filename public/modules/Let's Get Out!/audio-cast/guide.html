How Audio Cast works

1. CreateCreate one temporary session. LGO generates a session-specific QR code.
2. Visitors scanVisitors sign in, open My contents → My live guidings → Scan for a live session, then scan your QR.
3. One-way audioYour microphone is sent to connected listeners. Visitors cannot transmit audio back.
4. ControlMonitor listener IDs, connection quality and remove an unknown listener selectively.
5. RecoverIf your app closes unexpectedly, reopen Audio Cast to recover the same unended session. Visitors do not need a new QR.
6. EndEnding the session invalidates the QR permanently and disconnects all listeners.
