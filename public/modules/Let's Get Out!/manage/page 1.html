Guiding configuration

Choose the format of your guiding and how visitors will access it: Online (content on the platform), Live or IRL. You can reuse settings from an existing guiding later.

Step 1 of 2 — configuration • Step 2 — content & steps

Guiding type

Select how this guiding will be delivered. You will only use one type per guiding: Online, Live or IRL.

Only one type can be active for a given guiding. You can duplicate a guiding later to create alternative formats.

Online options

Content is uploaded to the platform. Visitors can follow it at their own pace according to your configuration.

EUR
If enabled, you will be asked to set up payout details in your guide profile.
When allowed, additional proceeds may come from subscriptions and downloads.

Live options

You will perform live remote sessions with visitors (video / audio / chat). The platform helps with presentation, calendar, messaging and payments.

IRL options

In-person guiding at a real location. The platform helps with presentation, messaging, calendar and secure payment.

Reuse configuration (optional)

If you already have guidings, you can duplicate their configuration as a starting point for this one.

When you reuse a guiding, only the configuration (type, monetization, options) is copied — content & steps will still be created in the next step.
